# DRAFT — Candidate-owned decision answers

Rewrite every answer in your own words. These prompts are notes, not submission-ready responses.

- 1. Most dangerous assumption: identify the assumption and the concrete risk if it is false.
- 2. What must never depend on an LLM, and why: discuss financial truth and deterministic checks.
- 3. When `UNKNOWN` is the correct answer: cite a case where an unsupported guess would be worse.
- 4. Hardest bug and verification: describe the failure, root cause, fix, and evidence it is fixed.
- 5. AI suggestion corrected or rejected: explain the suggestion, your reasoning, and the final choice.
- 6. First bottleneck at one million events per day: name the likely limit and how you would measure it.
- 7. One more day of work: prioritize one improvement and explain the tradeoff.
